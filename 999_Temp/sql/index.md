## SQL Mastery Roadmap: Table of Contents

---

### **I. The Fundamentals (DML)**

- **Data Retrieval:** `SELECT`, `FROM`, and Aliasing (`AS`).
    
- **Filtering Logic:** The `WHERE` clause and Comparison Operators.
    
- **Pattern Matching:** `LIKE`, `IN`, and `BETWEEN`.
    
- **Result Set Management:** `DISTINCT`, `ORDER BY`, and `LIMIT/TOP`.
    

### **II. Data Aggregation & Grouping**

- **Summary Functions:** `COUNT()`, `SUM()`, `AVG()`, `MIN()`, and `MAX()`.
    
- **Categorization:** The `GROUP BY` clause.
    
- **Post-Aggregation Filtering:** `HAVING` vs. `WHERE`.
    

### **III. Relational Data (The JOIN)**

- **The Big Four:** `INNER`, `LEFT`, `RIGHT`, and `FULL OUTER` Joins.

- **Advanced Connections:** `Self Joins` and `Cross Joins`.
    
- **Vertical Merging:** `UNION` vs. `UNION ALL`.
    

### **IV. Intermediate Logic & Subqueries**

- **Conditional Logic:** The `CASE` statement.
    
- **Nested Queries:** Subqueries in `SELECT`, `FROM`, and `WHERE`.
    
- **Query Readability:** Common Table Expressions (CTEs) using the `WITH` clause.
    

### **V. Advanced Analytics & Window Functions**

- **The OVER() Clause:** `PARTITION BY` and `ORDER BY` within windows.
    
- **Ranking & Sequencing:** `RANK()`, `DENSE_RANK()`, `ROW_NUMBER()`.
    
- **Offset Functions:** `LEAD()` and `LAG()` for trend analysis.
    
- **Data Cleaning:** String functions (`CONCAT`, `SUBSTR`) and `COALESCE` for NULL handling.
    

### **VI. Database Design & Optimization (DDL)**

- **Schema Management:** `CREATE`, `ALTER`, and `DROP`.
    
- **Data Integrity:** Primary Keys, Foreign Keys, and Constraints.
    
- **Normalizing Data:** From 1NF to 3NF.
    
- **Performance Tuning:** Understanding Indexes and Execution Plans.
